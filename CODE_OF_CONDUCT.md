# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We as members, contributors, and maintainers commit to making participation in
this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

We commit to acting and interacting in ways that contribute to an open,
welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Accept responsibility, apologise to those affected by mistakes, and learn from
  the experience.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users, not on winning an
  argument.
- Respect privacy. Do not share customer data, credentials, or private
  correspondence.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without explicit permission.
- Sharing production secrets, customer data, or another person's booking details.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They
may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and pull requests
that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate moderation
decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests,
discussions, commit messages, documentation, and any public space where someone
is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour privately to the
repository owner through the contact method on their GitHub profile. Do not open
a public issue for a conduct report.

All complaints will be reviewed promptly and fairly. Maintainers will respect the
privacy and security of the reporter. Enforcement actions are documented
internally; the reporter is informed of the outcome.

## Enforcement guidelines

| Level | Example | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Correction | Inappropriate language or a minor violation | Private warning with an explanation; a public apology may be requested |
| 2. Warning | A violation after a correction | Warning with consequences for continued behaviour; no interaction with those involved for a set period |
| 3. Temporary ban | A serious violation, or repeated violations | Temporary ban from interaction with the project |
| 4. Permanent ban | A pattern of violations, harassment, or aggression | Permanent ban from the project |
